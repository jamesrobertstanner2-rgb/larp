01 / COMMUNITY

Discord Rules

This section contains the official Discord rules for Los Angeles Roleplay. All members are expected to read and understand these guidelines before participating in the community.

⌕
01

Server Nicknames

Your Discord nickname must include your Roblox username for identification purposes.

02

Copying Server Assets

Do not steal or reuse server assets such as bot commands, custom embeds, or other features for your own server. Doing so may result in a permanent ban.

03

Community Respect

Treat everyone with respect. Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, or personal attacks will not be tolerated.

04

Obscene Content

All content must remain appropriate for all audiences. Graphic, sexual, violent, or otherwise obscene content is strictly forbidden.

05

Advertising

Advertising or promoting other communities is not allowed. This includes server invites, advertisements, or unsolicited DMs to members.

06

Language

Los Angeles Roleplay is a U.S.-based community, but players from all over are welcome. Please use English only in Discord and in-game chats.

07

Alternate Accounts

Alternate accounts are not permitted. Any attempt to bypass bans or moderation using alternate accounts will result in an immediate ban.

08

@ Mentions

Do not randomly mention members or staff without a valid reason. Random pings may result in a timeout.

09

Pointless Drama

Arguments, disruptive behaviour, or bringing drama from other communities is not allowed. Keep things positive and roleplay-focused.

10

Common Sense

Above all, use common sense. If something feels disrespectful, harmful, or unwise, don't do it.

!
MODERATION ACTION

Failure to follow these regulations may result in moderation action.

Depending on the severity and circumstances of the violation, moderation action may include a warning, timeout, kick, ban, or community blacklist.

02 / ROLEPLAY

Game Rules

The official game rules for Los Angeles Roleplay can be found below. Please take the time to read through them carefully to understand expectations and ensure all roleplay remains realistic, respectful, and enjoyable for everyone involved.

01

Roleplay Realistically

Always act in a way that makes sense in real life. Unrealistic driving, random actions, or trolling ruins the experience for everyone.

02

Follow Traffic Laws

Drive like you would on real roads. Stop at red lights, use indicators, and obey speed limits. Reckless driving or constant crashes may result in moderation action.

03

No Random Deathmatch (RDM)

Don't kill or attack other players without a valid roleplay reason. Every interaction should make sense in a real-life scenario.

04

No Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)

Do not use your vehicle to intentionally hit or kill other players. This includes running people over without a valid roleplay reason.

05

Respect Police Roleplay

If you are pulled over or arrested, cooperate with police roleplay. Running or fighting without a valid roleplay reason may be considered Fail RP.

06

No Fail Roleplay (FRP)

Don't break character or act unrealistically. For example, surviving being shot multiple times and immediately running away would be considered unrealistic roleplay.

07

No Cop Baiting

Don't purposely annoy or provoke law enforcement simply to start a pursuit. Keep interactions natural and realistic.

08

Respect Other Players

Treat everyone fairly and don't intentionally ruin another player's roleplay. Bullying, harassment, or toxic behaviour is not allowed.

09

No Exploiting or Glitch Abuse

Don't use bugs, glitches, exploits, or hacks to gain an unfair advantage. Report any discovered glitches to the Los Angeles Roleplay staff team instead of abusing them.

10

Use Common Sense

If you know something would not realistically happen, don't do it in-game. Consider how your actions may affect other players' roleplay.

!
ROLEPLAY ENFORCEMENT

These regulations apply whenever participating in the Los Angeles Roleplay in-game server.

Failure to follow these regulations may result in moderation action depending on the severity and circumstances of the violation.
